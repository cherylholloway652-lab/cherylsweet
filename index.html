Meet the heart

Every great pastry begins with a little imagination — and a whole lot of heart.

Cheryl is building Cheryl Sweet with a big vision: to create some of the most loved pastries in Houston and, one day, among the very best in Texas. With care in every detail and a love for bringing people together, each creation is an invitation to slow down, celebrate, and savour something special.

From everyday treats to unforgettable celebration desserts, the journey is just beginning — and there is always room for one more sweet moment.
